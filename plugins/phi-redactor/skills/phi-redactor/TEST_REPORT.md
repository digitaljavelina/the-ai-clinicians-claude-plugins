## 10. Caveats

- Recall is measured on one synthetic note template, so numbers prove the method and the pipeline, not that any real chart scores the same.
- Extraction coverage below 100% for a format reflects that format's extraction limits (PDF line-wrapping, office blind spots), not the redaction model.
- The reversible round trip used an AI step that preserved the tags; a real model that reworded them would show up as lost tags in section 6.
